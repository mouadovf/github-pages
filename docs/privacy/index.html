Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current privately operated My Ads Viewer integration and its connected Meta business tools.

Effective and last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Operator and scope

My Ads Viewer is operated by Mouad. Privacy contact: mouadbst07@gmail.com. The application is used to inspect and manage Meta assets that an authorized operator can access. It is not a public advertising marketplace or an application operated by Meta or OpenAI.

This policy covers the integration's handling of information. Meta, ChatGPT/OpenAI, the sign-in provider and the hosting provider also handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Information accessed and processed

Access depends on the permissions granted by Meta, asset assignments, and the operation requested. Information may include:

  • Business Portfolio, ad account, Facebook Page, professional Instagram account, pixel/dataset and catalog identifiers, names, relationships, access permissions and eligibility information.
  • Campaign, ad set, ad and creative information, including budgets, targeting, delivery status, schedules, account currency, billing/delivery restrictions and advertising performance statistics.
  • Facebook Page posts, comments and replies, their identifiers and timestamps, and author information only where Meta makes it available. Authorized Instagram profile and media information and catalog product information may also be read.
  • Images selected for the integration, creative text, links and other parameters provided for an advertising or Page operation.
  • Sign-in identifiers, granted permissions, authorization records and access credentials needed to connect to the services.
  • Operational records such as request times, requested parameters, errors, discovery results, action proposals, verification results and security/audit records.

Possessing a permission does not mean every data type available under that permission is collected. The integration does not provide general access to a person's Facebook password, private personal messages or all Facebook activity.

3. Purposes

The integration uses information to authenticate authorized users, discover accessible assets, answer requested reporting and diagnostic questions, retrieve Page content, prepare and carry out authorized supported actions, verify results, prevent duplicate operations, enforce authorization limits, and investigate errors or misuse. Access is limited by the permissions and controls in place; a request does not override Meta's access restrictions.

4. Storage and security

The integration runs on the operator's Mac. It stores an asset registry, relationships and permission evidence, cached comment/reply records, workflow and action history, and operational/security records locally. Selected images can be staged locally before an authorized upload.

Access credentials and staged image files are encrypted locally. Other operational database records are not all encrypted by the application; local access controls protect them. Protected backups may contain the same information. These measures do not guarantee absolute security.

Images are normalized before staging, removing embedded source metadata. Staging receipts expire after 24 hours. Expired receipts and image files no longer referenced by a current receipt are cleaned when another staging request runs; there is no independent timed purge that guarantees removal at exactly 24 hours. Upload acknowledgments and workflow records can remain after staged files are removed.

5. Service providers and disclosure

  • Meta: official Meta APIs provide authorized information and receive parameters, selected images and creative content when a supported Meta action is authorized.
  • OpenAI/ChatGPT: requested tool results are returned to the connected assistant. Selected attachments and relevant request information pass through the connected services. Information included in a conversation is subject to the user's applicable OpenAI service terms and settings.
  • Auth0/Okta and the selected identity provider: process sign-in and identity information used for authorized account access.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts only these public static information pages and their public assets. GitHub may record visitor IP addresses and request information for security and service operation. The integration's private records and credentials are not published on this site.

Processing by these providers may occur in countries other than your own. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to address misuse and protect the service. The operator does not sell the information collected by this integration.

Provider information: Meta Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy, Okta Privacy Policy, and GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Retention and deletion

Other than the limited staging cleanup described above, there is no general automatic purge schedule for asset records, cached comments, workflow history, logs, audit records or backups. They remain until manually reviewed and removed by the operator. Credential expiry or disconnection does not by itself delete previously stored data.

You can request deletion using the Data Deletion Instructions. The operator reviews requests, verifies the requester with proportionate information, removes applicable locally held data, and explains any information that must lawfully be retained. Backups and exported copies must be considered during that manual process; deletion from an active record does not automatically erase every backup.

Deleting local integration data does not automatically delete content, ads, images or account data held by Meta, or a separate ChatGPT conversation. Those services provide their own controls.

7. Your choices and requests

You can stop using the integration, disconnect its ChatGPT connection, or remove My Ads Viewer from the relevant Facebook Apps and Websites or Business Integrations settings. Removing access prevents further use of that grant, but a separate deletion request is needed for retained integration data. Do not revoke a business integration unless you intend to affect its connected functionality.

For access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law, contact mouadbst07@gmail.com. The operator will review and respond to the request. Never send passwords, access tokens, app secrets or payment details.

8. Public website and updates

These static pages contain no application analytics, advertising trackers, collection forms or application-set cookies. Hosting-provider logging is described above. The pages are informational and do not grant access to Meta assets.

The operator may update this policy to reflect changes to the integration. The date at the top identifies the current version. Questions can be sent to the privacy contact above.
