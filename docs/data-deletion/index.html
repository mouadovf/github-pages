Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask the operator to delete information held by the My Ads Viewer integration. Requests are handled manually.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Send a deletion request

Email mouadbst07@gmail.com with the subject My Ads Viewer — Data Deletion Request. This is the app's existing operator contact.

  1. State that you want information held by My Ads Viewer deleted.
  2. Provide the name or profile link that helps identify you, and a relevant Page, post or comment link if your request concerns Page content. An app-scoped user ID or asset ID can help if you already know it; it is not required to send the request.
  3. Describe the scope: for example, your cached comments, your connected-user information, or all personal information relating to you that the integration holds.
  4. Include a return email address for any clarification and the result.
Never send credentials. Do not include Facebook or Mac passwords, access tokens, app secrets, recovery codes or payment details. The operator may ask for proportionate evidence of your relationship to the requested data, through an appropriate channel.

2. How the request is handled

The operator reviews the request, identifies applicable local records and copies, verifies the requester where needed, and manually removes information that should be deleted. This can include cached Page comments and author identifiers, user/account-linking information, credentials, selected staged files, and relevant operational records, as applicable to the request.

Backups and exports must also be reviewed. There is no automatic deletion callback or self-service deletion endpoint on this website, and sending an email does not itself run a deletion job. The operator will respond with the outcome or any needed clarification. If limited information must lawfully be retained for security, legal or accountability reasons, the response will explain the scope and reason. No automatic or fixed-time complete erasure is promised.

3. Stop future access, if desired

You can independently remove My Ads Viewer through Facebook's Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites or Business Integrations, whichever lists the app. You can also disconnect the integration in ChatGPT. These actions may interrupt connected functionality and do not automatically erase records already stored by the integration.

You do not have to disconnect first to send a deletion request. Contact the operator if you need help identifying which grant applies.

4. What this does not delete

A request to remove local integration data does not automatically delete a Meta campaign, ad, uploaded image, Facebook post or comment from Meta itself, or remove an independent ChatGPT conversation. Use those services' controls or make a separate clearly scoped request to the appropriate service. Data removal will not be treated as permission to edit ads or delete public content.

The app's Privacy Policy explains what it handles, storage and retention, and the service providers involved. For questions or follow-up, contact mouadbst07@gmail.com.
